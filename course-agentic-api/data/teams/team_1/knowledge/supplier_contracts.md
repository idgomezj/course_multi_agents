# Supplier Contracts — Volatile Demand Division

SUP01 is the standard source for RM01, RM02 and RM05. SUP02 is normally cheaper for RM01 but has longer lead time. SUP03 provides short lead time for RM02, RM03, RM05 and RM06. SUP04 is an approved emergency source for all materials at a price premium.

Emergency sourcing should be compared on total cost, including lost-sales exposure and expedite premium. A cheaper supplier is not preferred when its expected arrival cannot protect the service commitment.

MOQ and commercial values are available through the supplier tools and remain authoritative.
