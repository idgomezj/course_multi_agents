# Capacity-Constrained Plant Policy

## Capacity
A production plan must respect line/product compatibility and available weekly hours. Nominal capacity must be adjusted for credible downtime risk and known labor constraints.

## Overtime
Overtime is allowed up to the configured weekly line limit. It should be used only when its total cost is preferable to late delivery, rescheduling or other feasible alternatives.

## Changeovers
Frequent product switching carries a material cost and reduces effective capacity. Plans should consolidate compatible runs when this does not violate service priorities.

## Maintenance
High downtime risk is a planning input. A line with elevated risk should not be loaded as if nominal hours were guaranteed.

## Customer service
Late-delivery penalties and service targets can justify overtime or alternate line assignment. The cheapest production schedule is not valid if it is infeasible.
