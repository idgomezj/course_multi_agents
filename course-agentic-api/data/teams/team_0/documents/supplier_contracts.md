# Team 0 Supplier Contracts

SUP01 is the standard source for RM01, RM02 and RM05. It has moderate lead time and strong reliability.

SUP03 is the preferred short-lead source for RM02, RM03, RM05 and RM06 when MOQ is satisfied.

SUP02 can be cheaper for selected materials but has longer lead time and lower reliability.

SUP04 is approved for every listed material and is the premium emergency source.

MOQ, unit cost, lead time and approved-material lists returned by the supplier tools are authoritative.
