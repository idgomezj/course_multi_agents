# Team 0 Operations Policy

## Objective
The reference plant must achieve at least 99% monthly service while minimizing total operational cost.

## Planning sequence
The Manager should retrieve relevant policy evidence, inspect finished-goods and raw-material inventory, inspect open purchase orders, forecast demand, calculate BOM requirements, compare authorized suppliers, build a feasible production schedule, compare total cost, and validate the candidate plan.

## Inventory
Inventory is permitted, but unnecessarily early purchasing increases holding and working-capital cost. Purchases should be timed close to material need when delivery risk permits.

## Supplier risk
The lowest unit price is not automatically the lowest total cost. For time-sensitive material, use supplier-delay evidence and compare downstream stockout or line-stop exposure.

## Service
A cheaper plan that fails the 99% service target is not preferred.

## Approval
Purchases above the configured approval threshold require the normal human approval workflow.
