# Supplier Contracts — Unreliable Supply Division

SUP01 is a standard supplier with strong quality performance. SUP02 may offer attractive prices but has historically shown greater delivery variability. SUP03 provides strong service for RM02, RM03, RM05 and RM06. SUP04 is the premium emergency source and is approved for all listed materials.

A quality alert must trigger explicit quality-risk evaluation before award. When two suppliers are both exposed to the same disruption, diversification only helps if the alternate risk is materially independent.

MOQ, lead time, unit cost and approved material lists are authoritative through the supplier tools.
