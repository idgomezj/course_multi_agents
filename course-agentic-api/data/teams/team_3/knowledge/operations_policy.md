# Just-in-Time Operations Policy

## JIT objective
The plant operates with minimal raw-material buffers. Inventory should be timed to production requirements rather than accumulated for convenience.

## Delivery timing
Before recommending a purchase, the planner must determine the required material date, usable on-hand quantity and open purchase orders expected before that date. Supplier delay probability and predicted arrival time are decision inputs.

## Micro-buffers
A small buffer is allowed only when its holding cost is economically justified by the expected cost of a production-line stop. Large generic safety stocks are inconsistent with this operation.

## Supplier choice
The lowest unit-price supplier is not automatically preferred. Delivery variance and the cost of a line stop can dominate unit price.

## Escalation
If no supplier can meet the material need date with acceptable risk, the Manager must consider expedite, production rescheduling or another feasible operational alternative.
