# JIT Supplier Service Agreements

SUP03 and SUP04 provide the shortest nominal lead times. SUP02 often offers lower unit cost but has wider historical delivery variability. Supplier reliability must be evaluated using the current trained models and recent scenario information.

For JIT materials, early delivery is not free: holding and working-capital charges apply from receipt. Expedite can shorten nominal lead time but incurs the configured per-unit premium.

MOQ remains binding even in JIT mode. If MOQ creates economically excessive stock, the Manager should compare a different authorized supplier or production timing alternative.
