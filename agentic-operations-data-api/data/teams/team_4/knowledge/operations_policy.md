# Unreliable Supply Network Policy

## Continuity
Critical-material availability and line continuity have priority over minimizing unit purchase price.

## Risk-adjusted sourcing
Supplier delay risk and quality-failure risk must be considered separately. A supplier can be punctual and still be unacceptable because of quality risk.

## Buffers
Strategic buffers are allowed for critical materials when expected disruption cost exceeds carrying cost. The buffer should be tied to risk and material criticality, not applied uniformly.

## Alternatives
When the primary supplier risk is elevated, evaluate authorized alternatives, dual sourcing, expedite and buffer strategies. Contracts and approved-supplier restrictions remain binding.

## Approval
High-value emergency purchases require human approval at the configured threshold.
