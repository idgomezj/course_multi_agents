# Volatile Demand Operations Policy

## Service
The business targets at least 98.5% monthly service. Promotions and priority-customer commitments must be protected unless a documented capacity or material constraint makes the target infeasible.

## Forecast selection
For stable weeks, simple forecasts may be used as a reference. When a promotion, material trend break, strong forecast disagreement, or unusually high recent variance is present, planning must consult the trained demand forecast and demand-uncertainty models before deciding safety buffers.

## Buffer policy
Inventory buffers must be economically justified. High uncertainty can justify a larger temporary buffer when expected lost-sales and emergency-procurement costs exceed holding cost. A buffer should be reduced after the event passes.

## Overrides
Confirmed customer orders are evidence, not automatically the full forecast. If confirmed orders and statistical forecast disagree materially, the plan must document the difference and evaluate downside risk.

## Approvals
Purchases above the configured approval threshold require explicit human approval before execution.
