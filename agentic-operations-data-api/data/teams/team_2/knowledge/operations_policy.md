# Stable Make-to-Stock Operations Policy

## Planning philosophy
Demand is mature and normally stable. The business prefers simple, robust forecasting over unnecessary model complexity. Plans should avoid reacting to short-term noise.

## Inventory
Excess finished-goods inventory is a major economic penalty. Existing inventory and open purchase orders must be checked before new production or purchasing is recommended.

## Working capital
Purchases that create inventory materially above expected four-week demand require economic justification. Working-capital cost and warehouse exposure must be considered together with unit purchase price.

## Service
The monthly target is 96.5%. Small buffers are acceptable, but large safety stocks are not the default.

## Approvals
Purchases above the configured approval threshold require human approval.
