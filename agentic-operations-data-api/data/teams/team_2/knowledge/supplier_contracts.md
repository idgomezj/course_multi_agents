# Supplier Agreements — Stable Make-to-Stock

Volume discounts may reduce unit price, but they do not override inventory and working-capital policy. A purchase should be evaluated using total landed and carrying cost over the planning horizon.

SUP01 and SUP03 are preferred routine suppliers when service and MOQ requirements are satisfied. SUP04 is approved but typically more expensive and should not be chosen only for faster lead time when no urgency exists.

Open purchase orders are firm commitments and must be included before recommending new orders.
